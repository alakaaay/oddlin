02What we collect

Information you give us

  • Quiz answers: the markets you trade, where you trade, how long and how often you trade, your typical position size, how your results are going, the contracts you usually buy, how you choose trades, tools you have used, what you value in a signal, how you want alerts, and whether you trade on a phone or a computer.
  • Email address, if you enter one, and your confirmation that you are 18 or older.
  • Choices on the offer screens: the plan you select and whether you add the Trade Audit. No payment is taken and no payment details are collected.

Information collected automatically

  • Device and browser information, IP address, pages viewed, the screen you are on, and the time of each action.
  • Identifiers set by the Meta Pixel (see section 5), and ad click identifiers in the page address when you arrive from a Meta ad.

We do not ask for your name, phone number, trading account details, wallet keys or payment cards.

03How we use it

  • To show your personalized feed setup inside the quiz.
  • To email you your feed setup and news about Oddlin’s launch, if you gave us your email. You can unsubscribe at any time.
  • To understand which questions, screens and offers work, so we can decide whether and what to build.
  • To measure and improve our ads on Facebook and Instagram, including showing ads to people who visited this site.
  • To keep the site secure and working.

We do not sell your email address. We do not use your answers to give you personal investment recommendations.

05Cookies and the Meta Pixel

This site uses the Meta Pixel, a piece of code from Meta. It sets cookies such as _fbp and sends Meta information about your visit and the actions you take here, so we can see which ads bring people to the quiz and show our ads to people who have visited. Meta may combine this with information it already has about you, under Meta’s own Privacy Policy.

You can limit this by blocking third-party cookies in your browser, using an ad or tracker blocker, or changing your ad preferences in your Facebook or Instagram settings under Accounts Center › Ad preferences.

06How long we keep it

  • Email address: until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it. If Oddlin does not launch, we delete all collected emails within 12 months after the beta ends.
  • Quiz answers and pixel events in Meta: kept by Meta under its own retention rules.
  • Hosting logs: kept by our hosting provider for a short period under its own rules.
